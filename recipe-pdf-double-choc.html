Double Chocolate Delight

⏱️ 40 minutes | 📊 Medium

The Story

For the true chocolate lover - an indulgent masterpiece that combines rich cocoa dough with chunks of premium dark chocolate. These cookies are intensely chocolatey, fudgy, and utterly irresistible.

🥄 Stage 1: Preparation

⏱️ 10 minutes

Gather Your Treasures:

  • 1¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup premium cocoa powder
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 cups dark chocolate chunks
↓

🥣 Stage 2: Dough Creation

⏱️ 15 minutes

The Magic Begins:

  1. Cream butter with both sugars until fluffy (4-5 minutes)
  2. Beat in eggs one at a time, then add vanilla
  3. Sift together flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt
  4. Gradually fold dry ingredients into wet mixture until just combined
  5. Gently fold in chocolate chunks
↓

🔥 Stage 3: Baking

⏱️ 10 minutes prep + 12 minutes bake

The Transformation:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
  2. Line baking sheets with parchment paper
  3. Scoop dough into 2-tablespoon portions, roll into balls
  4. Place 3 inches apart (they spread!)
  5. Bake 11-13 minutes until edges are set but centers look slightly underdone
↓

😋 Stage 4: Enjoying

The Perfect Moment:

  • Let cool on baking sheet for 5 minutes for fudgy texture
  • Best served warm with cold vanilla ice cream
  • Store in airtight container for up to 5 days
  • Pro tip: Slightly underbake for maximum fudginess - they firm up as they cool
